**Indian startups should treat ChatGPT Business Hub as a new business-information and measurement channel, not a replacement for their website, search optimisation or sales funnel.** Check eligibility, claim and verify your organisation if available, correct its public details, and establish a baseline for discovery and website clicks. Do not assume that claiming a profile guarantees recommendations or leads.

On **10 November 2026**, [OpenAI introduced Businesses in ChatGPT](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/), initially in beta. Its Business Hub lets eligible companies claim their business, maintain information and see aggregated insights about how people engage with them in ChatGPT. OpenAI also announced Business Agents, but those currently target eligible ecommerce and online-service businesses in the United States. That geographic limitation matters for founders in India.

## What exactly did OpenAI launch?

The announcement describes three distinct capabilities. First, eligible businesses can claim an organisation and update details through Business Hub. OpenAI says verified businesses can add self-service product feeds containing pricing and availability.

Second, Business Hub offers aggregated metrics, including mentions, website clicks and discovery topics associated with a company's website being cited. OpenAI says insights appear only after sufficient interaction volume, and businesses do not receive users' private conversations.

Third, eligible businesses can build a Business Agent that people may choose to talk to from a business profile. OpenAI says this agent experience is separate from the model's independent answer, and sponsored placements are labelled. **Business Agents are not a generally available India-wide feature**, so Indian founders should not build a launch plan around immediate access.

## Why this matters for an Indian founder

AI assistants add another interface where buyers may ask for comparisons before visiting a company's website.

The first question may be conversational rather than a conventional search query. If the assistant cites outdated pricing, a discontinued product or the wrong company description, a prospect may leave with the wrong impression.

The business opportunity is **accuracy at the moment of consideration**. But a new channel is not automatically a scalable acquisition engine. Its value depends on whether relevant buyers discover your company, click through and ultimately become qualified customers.

## A practical seven-day plan

### Day 1: Audit your public identity

Search for your company and principal products in ChatGPT using neutral, buyer-like questions. Record whether the company is mentioned, which sources are cited and whether the description is accurate. 

### Day 2: Check Business Hub eligibility

Review [OpenAI's launch announcement](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/) and follow its official Business Hub entry point. If your organisation is eligible, use the verification flow and review what information can be managed. If access is unavailable, record that limitation and continue with website improvements instead of trying unofficial shortcuts.

### Day 3: Make the website answer real buyer questions

Create or improve pages that explain who the product serves, what it does, where it operates, integrations, limitations and how customers can evaluate it. Use specific facts rather than inflated claims. ### Day 4: Fix technical discoverability

Confirm important pages return successful responses, have distinct titles, canonical URLs and descriptive headings, and are not accidentally blocked by robots rules or noindex directives. Keep XML sitemaps accurate and link relevant pages internally.

Avoid invented review scores, fabricated FAQs and markup for information visitors cannot actually see.

### Day 5: Establish a measurement baseline

If Business Hub insights are available, record mentions, website clicks and discovery topics. Separately track visits and qualified enquiries on your own website using privacy-respecting analytics. Do not equate a citation with a click, or a click with a customer.

### Day 6: Review what customers actually ask

Ask sales, support and customer-success teams which questions repeatedly delay a buying decision. Use those questions to improve documentation and comparison pages. For engineering-led companies, this might mean clearer API authentication, data residency, rate limits and integration guides. For service businesses, it may mean scope, process, eligibility and turnaround expectations.

### Day 7: Assign an owner and a review cadence

Give one person responsibility for checking company information monthly and after material product changes. Revisit the channel after enough data accumulates to support a decision.

## What should founders avoid?

Do not rush to manufacture hundreds of near-identical AI-optimised pages. They are unlikely to help buyers make decisions and can damage the credibility of your site. Do not publish claims about ChatGPT ranking factors that OpenAI has not disclosed. 

This is also an operational lesson: new AI interfaces create opportunities, but only when the underlying data is trustworthy. The same principle applies to engineering workflows. Our earlier article on [turning team conversations into reviewed code](/founders-guide/github-slack-teams-chat-to-code-startup-workflow/) explains why a faster interface still needs clear ownership and verification.

## The founder decision

**Start with a low-cost audit, not a new marketing department.** Check eligibility, correct public information and measure whether AI discovery brings relevant traffic. If it does, invest in better product explanations and customer evidence. If it does not, keep the fundamentals healthy and wait for a clearer signal.

OpenAI's November announcement makes business discovery inside ChatGPT more tangible. For Indian startups, the disciplined response is to be findable, accurate and measurable, while recognising that availability and commercial impact remain uncertain.
